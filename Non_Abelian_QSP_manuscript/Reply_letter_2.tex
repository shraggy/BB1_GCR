Dear Editors,

We are delighted to hear of the outcome from this supposedly final round of review through the external expert. We thank the expert for being extremely positive comment about our work in their comment "\textit{...I find the results quite spectacular. And I believe that it will have concrete applications in quantum control for many works to come. Yes, it seems to be a landmark paper.}" In particular we are excited that the revisions were helpful to the external expert in identifying the underlying interesting physics we wanted to highlight with our work, as the expert says, "\textit{It is very nice from the point of view of fundamental quantum physics because it's a rare example where the non-Abelian nature of the operators are put to use.}" 

Following we highlight our revisions as a feedback (blue) to the expert's comments (red). The related changes can be found in the revised manuscript attached with this re-submission, highlighted in blue.
In this revision, we first address the more important concerns of the referee in terms of figure legends.
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textcolor{red}{the title "Failure probability" in Fig. 4b should read "Success probability" instead. Likewise, "Cat State Infidelity" should read "Cat State Fidelity".} \textcolor{blue}{We sincerely apologize for the confusion, the figure legends are indeed correct, as expectedly, both infidelity and failure probability are decreasing with increasing cat size. However, the caption should read "Failure probability" and "Infidelity" instead of "Success probability" and "Fidelity", respectively. We have implemented this caption change now.}
    \item \textcolor{red}{In Fig. 5c(top), what is the x-axis? time? I don't understand this plot.} \textcolor{blue}{The plot shares its legend with the other two plots in Fig. 5(c), which is mentioned as Time($\mu$s). It has now been explicitly mentioned in the figure caption as well.}
\end{itemize}
We also wanted to address the referee's clause 

\textcolor{red}{"\textit{It is indeed quite hard to grasp} but after taking the time to do it, I find the results quite spectacular."} \textcolor{blue}{We have refined the abstract completely without changing or reducing the technical content to explain jargons before using them, as pedagogically as possible. In addition, we added a sentence related to our technique much earlier in the introduction to give the non-expert readers a hint at where we are going.}

With these changes in place, we hope to have addressed the expert's concerns and made the manuscript more accessible than before. We thank the external expert, the referees involved, and especially, the editors for taking the time to provide useful feedback through these reviews.
